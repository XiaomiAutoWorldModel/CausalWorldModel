04 / The approach

A shared architecture.
Many ways to shape the future.

01

Learn physical evolution

Mix general, driving, robot and rare-event data from pre-training.

02

Unify views and controls

One backbone links views and time.

03

Keep the world grounded

WorldRec 2.0 adds persistent 3D context; efficient attention enables streaming.

Three stages. One continuous foundation.

01 / Pre-train

Build a broad dynamic prior.

Single-view video learning on general and physical data.

1 view480pGeneral + physical data
02 / Mid-train

Make the future controllable.

Add multi-view data plus camera and world-control conditions.

1–7 viewsCamera-pose controlWorld control
03 / Post-train

Stream with fewer denoising steps.

Distillation for faster, stable long rollouts; per-scenario weights.

Few-step distillationLong-video rolloutsScenario-specific weights

Static and dynamic 3D Gaussians from multi-camera observations.
